The Gurukulam Programme

Seven and a half years in residence with your Gurus, studying Kalaripayattu, Ayurvedic scriptures and healing as one education, and leaving with a recognised qualification in Ayurveda.

The Legal Framework

A recognised path, for the first time since independence.

The Central Sanskrit University Act, 2020 created a legal route for a gurukulam to grant recognised qualifications in Ayurveda. Under Ordinance No. 44/2025 institutions can apply for affiliation as an Ayurveda Gurukulam, and under the NCISM Pre-Ayurveda Programme Regulations, 2024 students can move from gurukulam study into a BAMS degree.

GVS Ayurveda Gurukulam is in the process of obtaining affiliation with Central Sanskrit University under this framework.

How We Teach

Three streams, one education.

  • Śastra Kalarippayattu. The body learned from the inside, through years of practice.
  • Śāstra Veda, Tantra, Itihāsa, philosophy and dharma. The understanding of what the body is part of.
  • Swāsthyam Ayurveda and Kalari Chikitsa. Health, balance and healing.

Kalarippayattu teaches the body from the inside, which is what makes Kalari Chikitsa possible. Śāstra explains what the body is part of. A physician who has all three understands a patient in a way no textbook conveys.

Clinical Training

Where students practise.

A licensed Ayurvedic hospital operates on the Calicut campus today, treating patients while its bed capacity and clinical departments are expanded to the standard a medical college requires. Students will train where patients are actually treated, and the treatment improves because it is being taught.
