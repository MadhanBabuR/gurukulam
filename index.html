Our Story

1952

Hindustan Kalari Sangam

Founded in Calicut by Veerasree Sami Gurukkal, preserving the discipline of Kalaripayattu for a new generation.

2010

Kalarigram

Lakshman Gurukkal founds Kalarigram as an offshoot of Hindustan Kalari Sangam, carrying the tradition forward through training, Kalari Marma Chikitsa treatment, yoga, and meditation.

Today

GVS Ayurveda Gurukulam

We're building a residential Gurukulam in Calicut that unites three things under one roof: Kalaripayattu training, an Ayurveda medical college on the NCISM Gurukulam pathway, and an attached teaching hospital — reviving the classical model where tradition and healing were taught together.

Why now? The Government of India's push to revive Gurukula-style Ayurveda education gives this model a real academic pathway for the first time in decades. Why Calicut? It's where this tradition has lived for over 70 years — and where Kerala's own history of Ayurveda institution-building runs deepest.

What We're Building Toward

swāsthyam

Trained as a warrior, walking the path of dharma, a student arrives at swāsthyam. The Guru's grace turns it into vaidya śakti, the power to heal. — a teaching of Guru Veera Sri Sami Gurukkal, carried through the lineage

Every doctor we train will also be a practitioner.

Students here study three streams as one education:

  • Śastra Kalarippayattu. The body learned from the inside, through years of practice.
  • Śāstra Veda, Tantra, Itihāsa, philosophy and dharma. The understanding of what the body is part of.
  • Swāsthyam Ayurveda and Kalari Chikitsa. Health, balance and healing.

A physician who has trained his own body for a decade knows things about a patient's body that no textbook conveys. That is what a Gurukulam produces, and it is why these three were never taught apart.
